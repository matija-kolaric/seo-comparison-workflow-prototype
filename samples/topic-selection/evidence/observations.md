# Synthetic evidence fixture — not real research

All product names other than Seobility/Ahrefs, URLs, metrics, and ratings in this sample are invented for software testing. They must not be used in a real selection or article. The example.com URLs are placeholders, not sources that were visited.

This fixture represents ten organic ranking pages, one opened comparison page, and official-source checks for Seobility and the fictional Example Audit tool, all collected at 2026-08-30T00:00:00Z. The fictional buyer question is whether a managed-site audit workflow or a dedicated technical investigation better fits a small agency. The simulated gap is an absence of plan-limit context in inspected comparison pages. No actual gap, price, capability, search demand, or growth claim is made.

Its metrics demonstrate why reverse-order comparison keywords should use a maximum (90 vs 50), not a sum, and why the 10,000-volume alternatives keyword must be excluded entirely.
