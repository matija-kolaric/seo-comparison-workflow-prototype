## Method notes

- The standard prioritizes recurring characteristics across several sources over memorable phrasing from one page.
- Seobility-authored and current product pages outweigh guest-author stylistic habits.
- Source text is evidence for abstract style traits only. It is not a phrase bank.
- The source set should be reviewed when Seobility materially changes its editorial templates or product positioning.
