# SERP research and handoff gate

## Query scope

Analyze the top 10 current organic results for the primary query and each approved priority query. A priority query must represent a materially different reader question or decision cluster; do not analyze every keyword variation. Record market, language, device, retrieval time, data provider, and any personalization or collection limitation.

When DataForSEO is available, use it for normalized rankings, SERP features, volumes, and related-query discovery. Page inspection is still required because SERP data alone does not establish on-page coverage or quality.

## Per-result observations

For every result, record when retrievable:

- rank, URL, domain, page type, and publication/update date;
- rendered SERP title and snippet separately from HTML `<title>`, meta description, and H1 because search engines may rewrite them;
- exact, partial, or semantic use of the target query in prominent fields;
- comparison topics, answer format, pricing/freshness treatment, tables, FAQs, screenshots, and first-hand evidence;
- author/reviewer identity signals, source citations, and disclosure or methodology;
- relevant structured-data types and whether visible content appears consistent with them;
- direct-answer usefulness, crawlable textual coverage, originality, and source transparency.

Do not score speculative hidden ranking factors. For AI-search readiness, apply current official search guidance: crawlable and indexable content, helpful original information, clear sourcing/authorship, important information in text, useful images where appropriate, and structured data that matches visible content. Do not require special AI markup, artificial content chunking, or exact-match repetition.

## Gap analysis

Assign stable IDs such as `GAP-001`. For each gap, record:

- observed evidence across the analyzed results;
- the reader need it serves;
- proposed information gain;
- supporting claims or additional evidence required;
- priority: required, recommended, or optional.

A competitor omission is not automatically an opportunity. Reject gaps that are irrelevant, unsupported, duplicative, or included only to make the page longer.

## Human-experience evidence

Seek useful positive, mixed, and critical experiences when available rather than selecting only comments that support the planned positioning. Established review platforms can help discover recurring topics; public Reddit or LinkedIn posts can provide richer context. Do not bypass login, robots, paywall, or access restrictions. Preserve dates and product-plan context, and treat deleted or unverifiable posts as unavailable.

An anecdote can illustrate a workflow or concern. It cannot prove a feature, establish market consensus, or replace current official pricing and product documentation.

## Research gate

Pass only when:

- every priority query has 10 organic results or an explicit data limitation;
- query scope and exclusions are documented;
- SERP and on-page fields are not conflated;
- product facts use authoritative evidence;
- review anecdotes are labeled as experience rather than consensus;
- every recommended section maps to a reader need and gap, query, or supported claim;
- required, recommended, optional, and prohibited coverage is explicit.
