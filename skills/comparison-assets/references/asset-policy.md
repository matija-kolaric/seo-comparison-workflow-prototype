# Visual evidence policy

## Preferred sources

1. Current official product pages, documentation, or vendor-provided media.
2. Established review platforms when the image is necessary to discuss the review evidence and the page is publicly accessible.
3. Public Reddit, LinkedIn, or similar posts only when the individual post is itself editorially important.

For personal posts, prefer a concise paraphrase with a permalink. Use a screenshot only when context would be lost without it and the planned use is appropriate. Do not expose unrelated names, avatars, handles, comments, or other personal data. Never use private, deleted, access-controlled, or circumvention-obtained material.

## Accuracy and rights

- A screenshot documents what a source displayed on the capture date; it does not prove every statement shown.
- Preserve source context and do not imply that a reviewer, platform, or vendor endorses the article.
- Keep quotations short and necessary. Do not reproduce entire reviews or large portions of third-party pages.
- Record cropping, annotation, or privacy redaction. Do not alter product results or review sentiment.
- Provide attribution and a source link where publication format allows.
- Apply the caption, capture-date, link, and attribution requirements in `knowledge/seobility/citation-policy.md`; the manifest is the canonical source for the published caption.
- For this POC, the user's standing editorial direction authorizes routine inclusion of relevant screenshots from public official product pages without a separate manual approval. Use limited, contextual captures with source links, dates and accurate captions; do not silently omit all images solely because no explicit screenshot reuse license was found.
- Record user editorial authorization separately from vendor permission; never label an unverified license as granted or claim legal clearance. Explicit reuse restrictions, private information, access controls, misleading context or excessive reproduction still require a different asset or a linked-text fallback. Report a genuine unresolved blocker rather than treating it as ordinary screenshot approval.

## Image quality

Use a legible viewport and resolution, crop to the relevant interface or evidence, and avoid decorative screenshots. Alt text should describe the useful information rather than repeat the caption or stuff keywords.
